# M05 Per-contact statistics

Module documentation · Version ecog-basic/1 · 2026-10-30

## 1 Inputs

Per-contact voltage and stable source identity.

## 2 Processing

Calculate finite-sample count, mean, standard deviation, RMS and peak-to-peak range per contact.

## 3 Outputs

Per-contact statistics with units and separate nonfinite counts.

## 4 Boundaries

No PREP, LOF or bridging detection. Amplitude or contact names do not establish bad contacts or spatial adjacency.

## 5 Sources and review

Project implementations share existing intake and reporting infrastructure. Implementation and successful execution do not establish detection accuracy.
