# M06 Optional interval evidence

Module specification · ecog · 1.0.0rc3.dev7 · 2026-10-31

## 1 Input

At least ten seconds of calibrated continuous voltage with explicit source-channel and sample-clock identities. Select `fieldtrip_zvalue` explicitly; it is not enabled by the default recipe.

## 2 Processing

Call pinned FieldTrip `ft_artifact_zvalue` through the documented Octave compatibility runtime. Use absolute sample differences, cumulative=no and a research cutoff of 4; no band-pass, padding, spatial inference or scalp physiological-artifact thresholds. Retain the maximum channel z value and source sample intervals.

## 3 Output

`M06_fieldtrip_zvalue.json` records parameters, source versions, compatibility hashes and review intervals. One-based inclusive upstream samples become half-open recording-relative seconds and native source sample ranges. Preserve nonzero source origins. Interval candidates do not claim single-channel localization.

## 4 Interpretation

Pathological discharges and physiological transitions may trigger the feature. Candidates are not confirmed artifacts. No rejection, interpolation, reconstruction or quality score is performed. Missing runtime, short windows or degenerate input remain unassessed; no selection keeps M06 skipped.

## 5 Source and validation

[FieldTrip source](https://github.com/fieldtrip/fieldtrip) and [official artifact documentation](https://www.fieldtriptoolbox.org/tutorial/preproc/automatic_artifact_rejection/). Public-file execution and synthetic sample-coordinate tests are engineering evidence, not clinical accuracy or MATLAB equivalence.
