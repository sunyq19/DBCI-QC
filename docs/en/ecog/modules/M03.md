# M03 Basic signal integrity

Module documentation · Version ecog-basic/1 · 2026-10-30

## 1 Inputs

Voltage samples and native sample indices.

## 2 Processing

Locate nonfinite samples and runs of at least two exactly equal consecutive samples.

## 3 Outputs

Contact identity, native intervals, counts and candidate evidence.

## 4 Boundaries

Exact equality is a technical observation, not a validated artifact decision. No scalp amplitude or rejection-duration threshold is applied.

All samples are scanned; at most 10,000 detailed candidate locations are stored per window. Above that budget, full totals, per-contact counts and omitted counts are retained and the status is partial. Locations not stored must not be interpreted as normal.

## 5 Sources and review

Project implementations share existing intake and reporting infrastructure. Implementation and successful execution do not establish detection accuracy.
